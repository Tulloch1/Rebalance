Hello World!

                        
Welcome to the Portfolio Rebalancer. This section provides an overview of how the tool calculates asset weights, optimizes deposit allocations, and minimizes taxable events.

                    


                    

                        
Hello World!

                        
Dynamic Rebalance uses incoming deposits to proportionally top up underweight holdings, or directs withdrawals from overweight positions, without forcing unnecessary asset sales.

                    


                    

                        
Hello World!

                        
Complete Rebalance performs simultaneous buys and sells across your holdings to bring each position to its exact target weighting in a single session.

                    


                    

                        
Hello World!

                        
Minimum Transaction thresholds prevent tiny, fee-inefficient orders by consolidating sub-threshold amounts into the most underweight eligible asset.
